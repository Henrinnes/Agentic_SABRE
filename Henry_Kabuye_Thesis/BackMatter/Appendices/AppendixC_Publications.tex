\chapter{Publications Arising from This Thesis}
\label{app:publications}

The following peer-reviewed publications have arisen directly from the research
presented in this thesis.
Each publication corresponds to one of the three empirical chapters and is listed
with its full bibliographic details, status, and the chapter to which it corresponds.

\vspace{1em}

\noindent\textbf{Publication 1} \hfill \textit{Chapter 4}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent Kabuye, H., et al.\ (2025).
\textit{Explainable and Uncertainty-Aware AI-based Ransomware Detection}.
\textbf{IEEE Access}.
\textit{Published}.
\cite{kabuye2025explainable}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textit{Contribution:} This paper introduces a single-agent CNN framework with
Monte Carlo Dropout uncertainty quantification, CTGAN-based class-imbalance augmentation,
and SHAP/LIME post-hoc explainability for ransomware detection.
It establishes the uncertainty-aware detection baseline on which the subsequent
multi-agent studies build.

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Publication 2} \hfill \textit{Chapter 5}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent Kabuye, H., et al.\ (2025).
\textit{Agentic SABRE: An Uncertainty-Aware Neuro-Symbolic Multi-Agent Framework
for Adaptive Ransomware Detection}.
\textbf{Expert Systems with Applications (ESWA)}.
\textit{Accepted for publication}.
\cite{kabuye2026sabre}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textit{Contribution:} This paper introduces Agentic SABRE, a two-agent framework
decomposing ransomware detection into modality-specialised Semantic and Behavioural Agents
fused through a learned Decision Agent with an uncertainty-aware triage policy.
Key contributions include score-space CTGAN augmentation, temperature calibration,
Bayesian TPE threshold optimisation, and a counterfactual explanation protocol.

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Publication 3} \hfill \textit{Chapter 6}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent Kabuye, H., et al.\ (2025).
\textit{Extended Agentic SABRE: Knowledge-Augmented Three-Agent Ransomware Detection
with Peer Consultation and Continual Learning}.
\textbf{IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS)}.
\textit{Under review}.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textit{Contribution:} This paper extends Agentic SABRE with three principled
additions: a Knowledge Agent trained on the researcher-constructed RansKnow
threat-intelligence dataset; the Peer Consultation Protocol enabling inverse-uncertainty-weighted
inter-agent communication before fusion; and the Expert Feedback Loop providing
analyst-guided continual learning through reservoir-sampled experience replay.
