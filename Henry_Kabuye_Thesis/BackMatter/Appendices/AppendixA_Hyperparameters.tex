\chapter{Hyperparameter Configurations}
\label{app:hyperparameters}

This appendix documents the complete hyperparameter configurations used across all three
empirical studies.
Hyperparameters were selected by Bayesian optimisation with Tree-structured Parzen Estimator
(TPE) sampling via Optuna \cite{optuna2019} on the respective validation partitions, unless
stated otherwise as fixed architectural choices.

\section*{A.6\quad Temperature Calibration}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

Post-hoc temperature scaling \cite{guo2017calibration} was applied to all agent posterior
probabilities before fusion.
The calibration temperature $T_{\mathrm{cal}}$ was fitted by minimising the negative
log-likelihood on a held-out calibration partition (10\% of the training set, withheld
before any model training).

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Fitted temperature scaling values per agent and study.}
\label{tab:app_temp}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Study} & \textbf{Agent} & \textbf{$T_{\mathrm{cal}}$} \\
\midrule

Chapter~4 (ESWA) & Behavioural  & 1.34 \\
Chapter~5 (TIFS) & Semantic     & 1.12 \\
Chapter~5 (TIFS) & Behavioural  & 1.34 \\
Chapter~5 (TIFS) & Knowledge    & 1.08 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
